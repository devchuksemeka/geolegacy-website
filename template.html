What we do

Full-spectrum survey and geospatial services

One survey partner for every layer of investigation your project needs — from initial positioning to final engineering-ready reporting.

A

Land Survey

Topographic, cadastral and boundary surveys delivering precise, legally defensible spatial data for planning and title.

B

Hydrographic Survey

Bathymetric, seabed and waterway surveys for ports, dredging, pipelines and marine infrastructure projects.

C

Geophysical Survey

Sub-bottom profiling, magnetometry and seismic data acquisition to characterise the subsurface before you build.

D

Geotechnical Investigation

Soil borehole logging, sampling and lab-backed analysis that informs safe, cost-efficient foundation design.

E

Positioning, GIS & Remote Sensing

GNSS positioning, GIS mapping and satellite/drone remote sensing that turns coordinates into decisions.

F

Environmental & Data Studies

Soil, seabed and environmental baseline studies with full-cycle data acquisition, processing and interpretation.

How a project moves

From scope to delivered data

A consistent four-stage workflow keeps every survey on schedule, on standard and easy for your team to act on.

01

Scope & plan

We assess site conditions, define survey control and agree deliverables against your project's engineering requirements.

02

Acquire

Field and marine crews capture positioning, geophysical, geotechnical and hydrographic data using calibrated instrumentation.

03

Process & interpret

Data is cleaned, modelled and interpreted by our GIS and geoscience team into clear, usable outputs.

04

Deliver & support

You receive engineering-ready reports and maps, with consultancy support through design and construction.
